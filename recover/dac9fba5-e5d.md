## Phong Thủy & Cultural Gifting Context (Bối cảnh phong thủy & quà tặng)

> This section is what makes this guide distinctly Vietnamese rather than a generic global growing guide. The information below draws on Vietnamese folk beliefs, common Phong Thủy practice, and cultural gifting traditions. Treat it as a thoughtful cultural framing, not a hard rule — these are living traditions that vary by family and region.

### Ý nghĩa phong thủy (Phong Thủy significance)

Trong văn hóa dân gian và phong thủy Việt Nam, hoa mười giờ mang nhiều lớp ý nghĩa khiến nó đặc biệt phù hợp làm quà tặng:

- **Sự tỏa sáng và niềm vui**: Tập tính nở rộ lúc 10 giờ sáng, khép lại lúc chiều tối được ví như đón năng lượng tích cực của ngày mới — mang lại ánh sáng và sự lạc quan cho ngôi nhà.
- **Sự kiên cường và bền bỉ**: Một loài cây sống được trong đất nghèo, hạn hán, nắng gắt là biểu tượng của ý chí kiên cường — món quà ý nghĩa cho người bắt đầu công việc mới, vượt qua khó khăn, hoặc dọn vào nhà mới (tân gia).
- **Sự sung túc đa sắc**: Nhiều màu hoa (đỏ, hồng, vàng, cam, trắng, tím) tượng trưng cho sự phong phú, viên mãn — biểu tượng phong thủy phổ biến cho **tài lộc** và **an khang**.
- **Sống có chừng mực**: Mười giờ nở theo giờ — biểu tượng của nhịp sống điều độ, không tham lam hưởng tất cả, nhưng luôn rực rỡ khi đến lúc.

### Phase-by-phase detail (what to do, watch, and gift-relevance)

**Day 0 — Sowing day**
- **Visual:** Bare moist soil; tiny black seeds (~0.5 mm) on the surface.
- **Action:** Fill a shallow tray (khay ươm) with the standard 5:3:2 mix, **spray-bottle water until uniformly dark but not pooling**. Sprinkle seeds on top, **press lightly with a fingertip — do not bury** (Portulaca seeds need light to germinate) [citation:Johnny's Selected Seeds](https://www.johnnyseeds.com/growers-library/flowers/portulaca/portulaca-key-growing-information.html). Cover with clear plastic wrap or a dome; poke 2 toothpick holes for airflow.
- **Environment:** 22–26 °C (a warm balcony shelf works; avoid direct sun which cooks the seeds). Bright shade, not dark.
- **Watch out:** Over-watering → seeds rot in 2 days. Under-watering → no germination. The "moist brownie" texture is the goal.
- **Gift-relevance:**  None yet — but record the date, it's your countdown start.

**Day 3–5 — Germination**
- **Visual:** First **cotyledons** (round, fleshy, pale-green) push up like tiny apple-green pebbles. Germination rate is typically 60–85% [citation:Johnny's Selected Seeds](https://www.johnnyseeds.com/growers-library/flowers/portulaca/portulaca-key-growing-information.html).
- **Action:** **Remove the plastic dome** the moment you see green to prevent damping-off. Keep media **just slightly moist** — spray bottle, morning only.
- **Watch out:** **Damping off** (seedlings suddenly collapse at the base, go translucent) = fatal. Cause: fungus + too-wet + no airflow. Prevention: cinnamon dust on soil, or a single spray of diluted Trichoderma.
- **Gift-relevance:**  None yet.

**Day 7–10 — True leaves emerge**
- **Visual:** First **needle-like, cylindrical true leaves** (the signature Portulaca shape) appear in the center of each seedling. Plant is ~1–2 cm tall.
- **Action:** **Move the tray to dappled morning sun** (1–2 h direct, then bright shade). Thin out any extra-close seedlings — keep the strongest one per cell, or leave 2–3 if they're spaced ≥ 2 cm.
- **Watch out:** **Leggy seedlings** (long thin stems reaching up) = not enough light. Move closer to the sun immediately.
- **Gift-relevance:**  None yet.

**Day 14–21 (Week 3) — Seedling stage**
- **Visual:** A small green rosette, **2–3 cm tall**, with 2–3 true leaves. Stem is starting to look reddish at the base.
- **Action:** **Begin hardening off** — outdoor exposure 1 h → 2 h → 4 h over 3 days, gradually increasing. **First feeding:** ¼-strength NPK 20-20-20 (1 gram per 2 L water) OR **trà trùn quế** (1 part steeped worm-tea : 5 parts water) — once only. Mist AM only.
- **Watch out:** Sunburn — pale bleached spots on leaves = moved to full sun too fast. Move back to dappled for 2 more days.
- **Gift-relevance:**  None yet — but you can already start picking out the gift pot.

**Day 21–28 (Week 4) — Transplant & first pinch**
- **Visual:** Seedlings 3–5 cm tall, ready for their own pot. Roots are filling the cell.
- **Action:** **Transplant to 15–18 cm pot** with the standard mix. Water the new pot 1 hour before transplant. Make a hole with a chopstick, lift seedling by a leaf (not stem), settle in, firm soil, water once. **Hold fertilizer for 7 days** to let roots recover. **First pinch:** when the plant is ~7 cm tall, snip off the growing tip just above the 3rd true-leaf pair using clean scissors. This forces 2 side branches to form.
- **Watch out:** Transplant shock = wilting for 1–2 days. Normal. Keep in dappled shade for 2 days, then back to full sun.
- **Gift-relevance:**  The gift pot is now home — measure its diameter and confirm it matches the 15–18 cm "standard gift" row above.

**Day 28–35 (Week 5) — Branching**
- **Visual:** 2 side shoots visible at the pinch point. Plant is a **5–8 cm wide mound** with 3–5 branches.
- **Action:** **Optional second pinch** on the 2 new leaders for an even fuller mounded shape (highly recommended for gift presentation). Begin **½-strength NPK every 2 weeks** (1 g/L), or trà trùn quế weekly.
- **Watch out:** Yellowing lower leaves = over-watering. Let the top 2 cm dry fully before the next drink.
- **Gift-relevance:**  The silhouette is forming — this is the cheapest moment to add a top-dress of decorative pebbles (sỏi trắng, đá cuội) to dress the pot.

**Day 35–42 (Week 6) — Bud set**
- **Visual:** Tiny **pointed green buds** appear at the tips of mature branches. Plant is a tight 8–10 cm × 12–15 cm green ball.
- **Action:** **Move to full sun (6+ h/day)** — this is non-negotiable for bud conversion to flowers. **Switch feed to high-P, low-N** (NPK 10-30-10 at 1 g/L every 2 weeks) to push buds over leaves [citation:Proven Winners](https://www.provenwinners.com/learn/how-to/portulaca). Start nightly check for **rệp sáp** (white cottony tufts) and **ốc sên** (slimy trails, holes in leaves).
- **Watch out:** A **heatwave in Hà Nội (July) or Sài Gòn (April–May)** with >38 °C can cause bud drop — give 1–2 h of lưới cắt nắng 50% in the afternoon.
- **Gift-relevance:**  Buy / print the care card now. The plant is committed to blooming — countdown to handover begins.

**Day 42–49 (Week 7) — First flowers open 🎉**
- **Visual:** **First 1–3 flowers open** at mid-morning (8–11 a.m.), close by late afternoon. Plant is **5–10 cm tall × 10–15 cm wide**. The color reveals itself.
- **Action:** **Photograph the first flower** — useful for color ID if you want matching pots. Begin light deadheading (pinch off faded flowers 1×/week) to keep new buds coming. Continue high-P feed.
- **Watch out:** **Rain right at bud-open** = flowers turn to brown mush. If heavy rain is forecast, **temporarily move the pot under cover** that morning. The buds will re-open tomorrow.
- **Gift-relevance:**  **Still too small to gift** — but you can now confirm the exact color and decide on a matching wrapping.

**Day 49–56 (Week 8) — Bloom ramp**
- **Visual:** **5–15 flowers opening per day.** Plant is **10–15 cm tall × 15–20 cm wide** — a clearly blooming gift.
- **Action:** Deadhead 1×/week. Continue high-P feed. **Pinch any one stem that's reaching past the pot rim** to keep the mounded silhouette.
- **Watch out:** **Yellowing + no new buds** = likely root-bound (only in pots < 12 cm) or pH drift — flush with plain water once and resume ½-strength feed the following week.
